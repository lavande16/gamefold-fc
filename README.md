## 轻松开始一局 · Pick up and play

- **熟悉的 8-bit 游戏库：**通过卡片式游戏入口浏览自己的收藏，并可为内容整理封面，让游戏库更有个人味道。  
  **A familiar 8-bit library:** browse your own collection through game cards and add cover art to make the library feel personal.
- **按折叠屏握持方式安排按键：**触控按键采用 A/B 与 X/Y 的掌机式布局，适合横向游玩时双手操作。  
  **Controls made for handheld play:** the touch layout places A/B and X/Y buttons for two-handed landscape use.
- **从启动到进入游戏保持统一体验：**GameFold FC 使用配套的应用图标与启动封面，让打开应用、挑选游戏的过程更连贯。  
  **A consistent start:** the matching app icon and launch artwork carry the experience from opening the app to choosing a game.
- **为自己的游戏收藏准备内容：**本仓库及 APK 不附带 ROM、BIOS 或商业游戏文件。请自行导入你有权使用的内容；兼容性会因文件和设备而异。  
  **Bring your own collection:** this repository and APK do not include ROMs, BIOS files or commercial games. Add content you are entitled to use; compatibility varies by file and device.
